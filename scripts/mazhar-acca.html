    The most misguided thing I hear from other UK accountants is that ICAEW is "harder" than ACCA. I completed my ACA with ICAEW in 2006, and I had some of that superiority complex myself until I started working with ACCA students. It sounds a lot like people who sat O-levels saying GCSEs are easy, or moaning about A-level grade inflation and how things were tougher “back in their day”. Working directly with ACCA students changed my view, because I stopped judging from the outside and saw what they were being asked to do. What I know now: - ACCA has stronger global recognition - the exams are comparable in difficulty - access to each, and the conditions around it, are very different At KPMG, everyone around me doing ACA was a graduate. The firm paid for every course and gave them loads of study leave. That was my world, so I assumed it was the hardest path. Then I started tutoring ACCA students: - most are self-funding - very few get any study leave - full-time work is normal - many have children and family responsibilities too All of that whilst sitting the same level of qualification. Getting ACCA under those conditions is far more impressive than anything ACA ever asked of me. So when an accountant tells me ICAEW is superior, my reaction is simple: “you don't know ACCA”. If you're balancing everything and pushing through sitting after sitting, what you're doing is genuinely remarkable!

    What is Compliance and Due Diligence in UAE Business? In the dynamic UAE business landscape, Compliance and Due Diligence are essential pillars for sustainable growth, risk mitigation, and regulatory adherence. Compliance refers to the ongoing process of adhering to all applicable UAE laws, regulations, and standards. This includes: 1. Corporate governance under Federal Decree-Law of 2021 Anti-Money Laundering (AML) & Counter-Terrorism Financing (CFT) requirements 2. Corporate Tax (9%), VAT filings, and Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) disclosures 3. Licensing renewals, labor laws, data protection (PDPL), and sector-specific rules in free zones, DIFC, or ADGM. It is not a one-time activity but a continuous discipline to avoid hefty fines, license suspension, or reputational damage. Due Diligence is the thorough investigation conducted before entering partnerships, acquisitions, investments, or onboarding clients. It involves legal, financial, regulatory, operational, and risk assessments — including Customer Due Diligence (CDD), Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) for high-risk cases, and verification of beneficial owners. In the UAE, robust due diligence protects against hidden liabilities, ensures AML compliance, and supports informed decision-making in a multi-jurisdictional environment. Why it matters: Strong compliance and due diligence build trust with regulators, investors, and partners — unlocking opportunities in one of the world's most business-friendly markets while safeguarding your operations.
